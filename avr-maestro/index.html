FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is AVR Maestro affiliated with Denon or Marantz? +
No. AVR Maestro is an independent, third-party application developed by MiyaraHub Technologies LLC. It is not created by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Denon, Marantz, or Sound United.
Which receivers are supported? +
Networked Denon and Marantz AV receivers with IP control (HTTP and/or Telnet) - including Cinema 30/40/50, AV10, the X-series (X1800H through X6800H and up), and SR/NR models. If your receiver is network-enabled, it's very likely supported.
How does it communicate with my receiver? +
Over your local network. AVR Maestro uses HTTP for reliable status polling and commands, and Telnet for live event subscriptions - so the app stays in sync with the physical remote and front panel.
Does it support Audyssey, Dirac Live, and IMAX Enhanced? +
Yes. Full control over Audyssey MultEQ, Dynamic EQ, Dynamic Volume, LFC, and Containment; Dirac Live filter-slot switching; and IMAX Enhanced with crossover settings - on the chassis that support them.
Can I control multiple zones? +
Yes. Zone 2 and Zone 3 each get independent power, volume, input source, and quick presets.
What are Scenes? +
A Scene is a one-tap snapshot of your whole setup - input, volume, sound mode, Audyssey/Dirac selection, speaker preset, channel levels, and zones - saved and recalled instantly.
How much does it cost? +
A one-time purchase. No subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases. Buy it once, on the platform of your choice.
Which platforms are supported? +
iPhone and iPad (App Store), Android (Google Play), Windows (Microsoft Store), and macOS (Mac App Store).
