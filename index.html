The studio

Small studio. Careful work.

MiyaraHub Technologies is a small, independent LLC in Dallas, Texas, owned and run by Meir Miyara.

We're not a factory and we don't pretend to be. We're a small team that cares about getting the details right - the kind of software you'd want on your own devices, controlling your own gear.

We build in the open, listen to the people who use our apps, and fix things fast. A lot of what ships in a release started as a message from someone in the community - and we'd rather it stayed that way.

We won't oversell. Some of our apps are polished and shipping to thousands of people; a few are still on the workbench, and we'll tell you which is which. What we promise is honest effort, native software, on-device by default, and no subscriptions for things that run on hardware you already own.
