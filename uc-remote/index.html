How it works

Direct to your remote. No cloud required.

UC Remote pairs with your Unfolded Circle remote on your own Wi-Fi and talks to it directly, using the remote's own integration interface. Your setup never touches an external server.

1

Pair Your Remote

Enter your Unfolded Circle Remote Two or Remote 3's IP address and the PIN it shows. UC Remote pairs securely and pulls in your profiles, pages, activities and entities.

2

Stay In Sync

A live two-way connection streams state from the remote - what's on, what's playing - while your taps go straight back as commands. No polling lag, no cloud round-trip.

3

Control Everything

Run activities, work the custom pages, drive every entity and media player, switch between multiple remotes, and pin widgets to your home screen - all from one app.

Security & Privacy

Local only. No account. No cloud.

UC Remote is built privacy-first and local-only: no cloud dependency, no user account, and no data collection beyond crash reporting.

◉

No Cloud Relay

Every command and the live state stream happen directly between your phone and your remote over your local Wi-Fi. Nothing leaves your network.

◎

No Account Required

No sign-up, no email, no MiyaraHub account. Pair with your remote's PIN and you're in control.

▣

PIN-Paired Access

Pairing exchanges a secure access token that is stored locally on your device and used only to talk to your remote.

◆

Local Storage Only

Remote addresses and app preferences are stored on your device. Nothing is synced to a server.

▢

Your Actions Only

Commands are sent only when you act. Nothing fires automatically or in the background without your knowledge.

☰

Network Permission Only

The only permission UC Remote needs is local network access, used solely to reach your remote on your Wi-Fi.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about UC Remote.

Is UC Remote affiliated with Unfolded Circle? +
No. UC Remote is an independent, third-party companion app developed by MiyaraHub Technologies LLC. It is not created, endorsed, sponsored, or affiliated with Unfolded Circle in any way. "Unfolded Circle," "Remote Two," and "Remote 3" are trademarks of their respective owners; our use of these names is solely to describe compatibility.
Which devices does it work with? +
UC Remote works with the Unfolded Circle Remote Two and Remote 3 on your local network. If your remote is reachable on the same Wi-Fi as your phone, UC Remote can pair with it and mirror its activities, pages and entities.
How does it connect to my remote? +
Over your local Wi-Fi. After a one-time PIN pairing, UC Remote keeps a live event channel open to stay in sync with your remote's state and sends your commands directly. There is no cloud relay and no account - everything stays on your network.
Do I need an account or an internet connection? +
No account, ever. UC Remote only needs your local network to reach your remote - nothing leaves your Wi-Fi, and there is no cloud service to sign up for.
Can I control more than one remote? +
Yes. Pair as many Unfolded Circle remotes as you like and switch between them in a tap. Each remote keeps its own profiles, pages and entities.
What platforms is UC Remote available on? +
UC Remote is a native app for Android phones and tablets, available on Google Play.
